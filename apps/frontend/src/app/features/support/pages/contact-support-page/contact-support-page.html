Quick Answers Is my transaction data safe? Yes. All transaction data is encrypted and securely stored. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information. Can I export my transaction history? Yes. You can export your data anytime for personal analysis. Go to Upload Files in the dashboard. You can filter by your desired date range or specific accounts, select your preferred file format and click the Download button to save the file directly to your device. How do I delete my account and permanently wipe my data? We take your privacy seriously. If you choose to leave, navigate to Profile Settings > Personal Info > Delete All Personal Data.

Once you confirm your identity via email verification, your profile, linked credentials and entire transaction history are permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.
