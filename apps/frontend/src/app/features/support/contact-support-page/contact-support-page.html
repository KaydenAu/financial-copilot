Quick Answers

Is my transaction data safe?

Yes. All transaction data is encrypted and securely stored. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

How do I link my bank account?

Go to Settings → Linked Accounts and follow the connection steps. Supported banks can be linked securely in just a few minutes.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription anytime from the Billing section. Your access will remain active until the end of the billing period.
